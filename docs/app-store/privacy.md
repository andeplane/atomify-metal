# Atomify privacy policy

Effective November 14, 2026. Applies to Atomify 3.0 for iPhone and iPad.

Atomify runs its bundled molecular dynamics simulations on your device. It does
not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal
data. It includes no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

The app stores its settings and temporary simulation working files locally on
your device. These are used to operate the app and are not sent to us. Removing
the app removes its local app data, subject to your device's backup settings.

Links to the source code, support and LAMMPS open in your browser. Those websites
have their own privacy policies. Apple may process App Store purchases,
downloads and diagnostics according to your Apple account and device settings;
this policy describes the Atomify app itself.

For questions, contact the developer through
[Atomify support](https://github.com/andeplane/atomify-metal/issues).

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
